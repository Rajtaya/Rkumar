Research interests

Primary: automata theory (pushdown and multi-pushdown systems, formal languages), computational complexity (Turing-machine complexity, complexity of language operations), and formal verification (context-, round-, and scope-bounded reachability). Additional: search-based software engineering, genetic algorithms and metaheuristics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics. Current directions (ongoing, unpublished): multi-pushdown systems and bounded reachability.
